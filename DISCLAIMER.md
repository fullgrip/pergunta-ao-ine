## English

**Independent project.** Pergunta ao INE ("Ask INE") is an open-source project by FullGrip.ai. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by Statistics Portugal (Instituto Nacional de Estatística, INE). The name "INE" only identifies the data source.

**The data.** Values come from INE's public API and remain subject to INE's reuse and attribution terms. They may be out of date, revised by INE after retrieval, or missing for some municipalities and periods. When the app uses stored values ("snapshot"), it shows the date they were collected. The official source, linked in every answer, always prevails over what the app shows.

**Explanations are written by AI.** The explanatory text is generated by the Gemma 4 model (locally through Ollama, or through Google's Gemini API). The app checks the numbers and rejects statements it cannot verify, but the text may still contain errors, simplifications or interpretations that are not INE's. Explanations are not official statistics.

**Illustrative examples.** Figures such as "an 80 m² flat" or "per 100 people" are illustrations built from medians and rates. They are not the price, value or situation of any specific case.

**Not advice.** The information is for learning about and exploring public data. It is not financial, real-estate, legal, tax, safety or any other professional advice. Do not make decisions (renting, buying, investing, moving) based only on this app; check the official source and, where appropriate, a professional.

**Privacy.** Questions are sent to the configured model: Ollama on the same computer, or Google if the Gemini API is enabled (subject to Google's terms and privacy policy). Do not type personal data into questions. The app only stores preferences in the browser (municipality, language, style).
